Loomwork AI, Inc. - Founder Restricted Stock Agreement (excerpt)
This Restricted Stock Agreement is entered into as of November 3, 2025 (the "Grant Date") between Loomwork AI, Inc., a
Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and Nathan J. Brooks (the "Founder").
1. Issuance. In consideration of services rendered and to be rendered, the Company issues to Founder 20,000 shares of
Common Stock (the "Shares"). No cash purchase price is payable. The Board has determined the fair market value of the
Common Stock on the Grant Date to be $0.40 per share (aggregate $8,000).
2. Vesting. 25% of the Shares vest on the first anniversary of the Grant Date (11/03/2026); the balance vests in 36 equal
monthly installments thereafter, subject to Founder's continuous service. Unvested Shares are subject to forfeiture on
termination of service.
3. Section 83(b) Election. Founder understands that he may elect under Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code to be
taxed currently on the fair market value of the Shares. Such election must be filed with the IRS within thirty (30) days
after the Grant Date. Founder acknowledges that it is Founder's sole responsibility, and not the Company's, to timely file
the election.
Signed: Priya Ramaswamy, CEO, Loomwork AI, Inc. / Nathan J. Brooks, Founder - 11/03/2025
