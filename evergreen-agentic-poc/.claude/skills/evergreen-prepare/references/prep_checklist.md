# Preparation checklist (Project Evergreen SOP v2, turned into questions)

Go through every section the return touches. Each bullet is a question to answer *with evidence*.

## A. Filing status and dependents (SOP: Filing Status, Kid Taxes)
* Marital status at 12/31? Death in 2025 (survivor files MFJ)? Married late in the year (MFJ allowed; also run MFS)? Divorce final by 12/31? Separated -> "considered unmarried" HOH test?
* Single filer with a qualifying person -> HOH? Who is custodial; was Form 8332 signed (CTC goes to the noncustodial parent, HOH/2441 stay)?
* Each dependent: age at 12/31, SSN, months in home, qualifying child vs relative ($5,200 gross-income test; nontaxable SS excluded), citizenship/residency (nonresident-alien parents can't be dependents unless US/Canada/Mexico residents), newborns.
* Child with unearned income > $2,700 -> Form 8615 own return (new client ID per SOP) or Form 8814 (interest/dividends only).

## B. Wages and compensation
* Duplicates removed? Excess SS with two employers? Additional Medicare not withheld?
* Box 12/14: equity comp (fix broker basis - code B), dependent-care FSA, HSA code W, Roth vs pre-tax, overtime (premium only for Sch 1-A), tips (W-2 tips, allocated tips vs actual, Form 4137), 2% S-corp shareholder health insurance.
* Restricted stock: 83(b) elections - timely (30 days from grant, Form 15620)? If late, no grant-year income. ISO exercise-and-hold -> AMT.
* Household employees (nanny) -> Schedule H, not a 1099 contractor.

## C. Interest and dividends (SOP: Schedule B)
* Accrued interest paid at purchase reversed? Bond premium (box 11/12/13) netted? Market discount (code D) moved to interest? US Treasury interest (state subtraction)? Tax-exempt interest on line 2a (affects SS taxability)? Nondividend distributions reduce basis? 199A dividends -> QBI? Foreign tax -> FTC route? Seller-financed note interest (payer name/SSN/address)? Foreign bank interest (convert; taxable even if tax-free abroad)?

## D. Capital gains (SOP: Schedule D, 8949 decision tree)
* Covered with no adjustments -> summary line; otherwise detail on 8949 with codes (B, W, D, C, H, M); complex ones grouped with a PDF statement.
* Missing/zero basis: find it (cost records, vest FMV, inheritance date-of-death value - community property gets a full step-up, gift carryover basis).
* Wash sales the broker can't see (spouse, IRA - permanent disallowance). Crypto: no wash sale in 2025; self-transfers aren't sales; 1099-DA vs crypto-tax report double counting; NFTs may be collectibles.
* Capital loss carryover by character (ST/LT) - prior preparer's worksheet may be wrong.
* Home sale: §121 ($250k/$500k; surviving spouse within 2 years gets $500k), depreciation recapture after home office.
* Installment sales: Form 6252 gross-profit % x principal received in 2025 only; interest separately.
* Constructive sales (§1259), reorganisation boot (§356), liquidations (§331), worthless stock (§165(g) - last day of year), §1244 ordinary loss ($50k/$100k MFJ).

## E0. Organizer lines that lump different kinds of costs together
* Deduct the clearly deductible part, leave out the clearly non-deductible part (e.g. car washes under standard mileage), make a reasonable documented estimate only if the documents support one, and ask the client for the split. Don't deduct the whole lump "pending a split".

## E. Schedule C (SOP: Schedule C)
* Cash basis for tax (convert accrual books: A/R, A/P changes). Personal expenses out; meals 50%; gifts $25/person; club dues out; de minimis safe harbor ($2,500) vs capitalize/depreciate (bonus/179); vehicle - standard mileage OR actual, not both, commuting excluded.
* Prior-year AJEs booked? Tax-prep fee proration to Sch C. 1099s filed for contractors (> $600; "did you file" box)? New business box?
* Home office: Form 8829 vs simplified ($5/sq ft, 300 max) - pick larger; remove the office share of mortgage interest/taxes from Sch A.
* SEP maximum (20% of net SE earnings after 1/2 SE tax) if client wants it, funded by extended due date. SE health insurance - not allowed in any month eligible for an employer-subsidised plan (including spouse's).
* Gross receipts: reconcile 1099-NEC, 1099-K and bank deposits - count income once.
* QBI: reduce by 1/2 SE tax, SE health, SEP.

## F. Schedule E - rentals (SOP: Rental Properties)
* 1098 with the rental address -> Schedule E. Repairs vs improvements (capitalize roofs etc.; de minimis election); depreciation from the schedule; land excluded.
* Personal-use days: > greater of 14 days or 10% of rental days -> §280A residence: expenses limited to rental income, allocated by days (IRS method; Bolton alternative), carryovers.
* Short-term rental (average stay <= 7 days): not a rental activity - material participation test decides passive vs nonpassive; transient-use unit is 39-year property.
* Passive: $25k allowance phase-out $100k-$150k MAGI; prior-year suspended losses (organizer often blank - use PY 8582); LPs get no allowance; passive income from other activities frees losses; PTP losses only against the same PTP.
* Involuntary conversion (§1033): deferral election, replacement deadline = end of 2nd year after the gain year (3rd for condemned business/investment real property).

## G. K-1s (SOP: Schedules K-1)
* Basis -> at-risk -> passive, in that order (Form 7203 for S corps: stock basis and debt basis separately; distributions test stock basis only - excess is capital gain).
* Partnership: outside basis incl. liability share; §752(b) decrease = deemed distribution; distributions above basis = gain (§731); §751 hot assets; §704(c)(1)(B) / §737 when contributed property is distributed within 7 years (often only in a footnote).
* §311(b) property distributions from S corps (gain may be missing from the K-1), built-in gains.
* Trust K-1s: final-year items (excess deductions, capital loss carryovers), distributed passive property (§469(j)(12) - suspended losses added to basis, not deducted).
* PTET: entity-level tax is not a Sch A deduction; claim the state credit. SSTB flag; 199A W-2/UBIA. K-1 state schedules -> nonresident filing requirements (ignore immaterial/loss-only states).

## H. Retirement, Social Security, other income
* QCD (70½), rollovers, Roth conversions (8606 pro-rata with year-end traditional/SEP/SIMPLE balances), NUA (taxable = cost basis), 10% early-distribution exceptions (55 separation, QDRO, etc.), inherited IRA rules.
* SSA-1099 net benefits; Medicare premiums -> Sch A medical; foreign pensions (convert; may count as SS under a treaty).
* State refund (tax-benefit rule), unemployment, gambling (W-2G), 1099-K personal items, staking/airdrops (Sch 1 line 8v), alimony (pre-2019 only), cancelled debt (insolvency/Form 982 + attribute reduction).

## I. Adjustments and deductions (SOP: Schedule A)
* HSA (code W already counts; excess?), educator expenses, student loan interest, IRA deduction limits.
* Medical: 7.5% floor; decedent's final medical paid within a year (§213(c)).
* SALT: actually paid in 2025; income vs sales tax (+ large-item sales tax); $40k cap with phase-down; PTE tax excluded; prior-year refund taxability.
* Mortgage: $750k/$1M limit by average balance; rental/home-office share removed.
* Charity: DAF yes, GoFundMe no; clothing at thrift FMV; substantiation; AGI limits. Gambling losses up to winnings. IRD §691(c). Casualty only if federally declared.
* Compare itemized vs standard (+ age boxes) and record both.

## J. Credits and other taxes
* CTC/ODC (MAGI phase-out), 2441 (FSA reduces base; overnight camp excluded), education (scholarships reduce expenses; phase-out), EITC (investment income limit), energy credits (2025 expenditures; EV acquired by 09/30/2025), FTC (de minimis vs 1116, treaty-rate limit, carryovers), saver's credit.
* SE tax, Additional Medicare, NIIT (investment interest allocation; active S-corp gain exclusion via 8960 line 5c), AMT (ISO, SALT add-back, limited-loss items), Schedule H, 5329, excess APTC (Form 8962), §962 tax, kiddie tax.

## K. Foreign (SOP: Foreign Transactions)
* FBAR (signature authority counts; max balances at Treasury year-end rate), 8938, 3520 (gifts > $100k from foreign individuals; foreign trust distributions), 5471/8865/8621, FX at IRS yearly average for income.

## L. Payments, penalties, extensions (SOP: Extension Prep)
* Withholding ties to forms (incl. 1099-R, W-2G, 1042-S, Additional Medicare); estimates actually paid (dates!); prior-year overpayment applied; extension payment; Form 2210 (lesser of 90% / 100%-110%); 2026 estimate recommendation (110% if 2025 AGI > $150k).

## M. States and locals (SOP: SALT Implications, States)
* Wage base per state (box 16 for the state shown vs box 1 for others; deferrals; severance/vacation sourcing) reconciled before applying any workday ratio.
* Before listing a nonresident return, test that state's filing threshold / de minimis day rule / reciprocity - and list the returns you decided NOT to file, with the reason.
* Residency all year; part-year moves; nonresident sources (workdays - check reciprocity, de minimis days, convenience rules; K-1 apportionment; gambling location; rental location); resident credit for taxes paid to other states; local city returns; state modifications (US interest, retirement exclusions, bonus addbacks).
