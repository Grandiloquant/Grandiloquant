# Reviewer checklist (SOP: Review section + the silent-error list)

## Always
- [ ] Every PBC document tickmarked used/excluded with a reason; duplicates, superseded, drafts, informational, other people's forms identified
- [ ] Prior-year recurring items all accounted for (blank organizer line ≠ zero); prior-year carryovers brought in (capital loss by character, passive regular/AMT, 8606 basis, AMT credit, FTC, charity, QBI loss, 6252 GP%)
- [ ] Filing status correct for 12/31 facts (death, marriage, divorce, separation/HOH, QSS only in later years)
- [ ] Dependents: ages at 12/31, SSN, residency, 8332 releases, newborns, qualifying-relative income test; kiddie-tax returns
- [ ] Withholding and estimates tie to source (dates matter; Q4 paid in January counts for 2025; extension payment; PY overpayment)
- [ ] Engine diagnostics cleared/explained; e-file blockers identified
- [ ] Deduction choice (itemized vs standard incl. age boxes) optimal; Schedule 1-A items eligible (premium-only OT, qualified tips occupation, new US-assembled vehicle, 65+ by 12/31)
- [ ] Analytical review vs prior year; effective rate sane

## High-risk areas - re-derive, don't just read
- [ ] Equity compensation basis (RSU/NSO/ESPP); ISO AMT; 83(b) timeliness
- [ ] Wash sales across accounts (spouse, IRA); crypto (double counting 1099-DA vs tax report; transfers; staking once)
- [ ] Accrued interest / bond premium / market discount; tax-exempt interest placement
- [ ] Retirement distributions: QCD age, rollovers, conversions (8606 pro-rata), NUA, early-distribution exceptions, corrected 1099-R
- [ ] Rental: 1098 address, repairs vs improvements, personal-use days (§280A), short-term rental status, passive limits
- [ ] Schedule C: cash vs accrual, personal items, meals/gifts/dues, home office double counting with Sch A, SE health eligibility, gross receipts double counting, SEP math
- [ ] K-1s: basis/at-risk/passive ordering; S-corp stock vs debt basis; distributions above basis; footnote items (§704(c)(1)(B), §311(b), §752(b)); final trust K-1s; PTET
- [ ] SALT paid-in-year, cap and phase-down; mortgage $750k/$1M; charity limits & substantiation; gambling 100% (2025)
- [ ] Credits: CTC phase-out, 2441 FSA offset, AOTC scholarships/phase-out, EITC investment income, FTC route (de minimis only for qualified passive income on payee statements; treaty-rate limit), energy credit dates
- [ ] Other taxes: SE, Additional Medicare (not withheld across employers), NIIT (MAGI excess; investment interest), AMT, Schedule H, 5329, excess APTC
- [ ] Foreign: FBAR/8938/3520/5471/8865/8621 triggers and FX rates

## States and locals
- [ ] Residency/part-year; nonresident sources (workdays, K-1 schedules, gambling, rentals); reciprocity & de minimis; resident credit
- [ ] City returns (Detroit, Grand Rapids...), part-year city allocation
- [ ] State modifications (US interest, retirement exclusions, bonus addback, PTET credit)

## Hand-off readiness (SOP: Hand-Off to Signer)
- [ ] Every required filing listed with method (e-file/paper) and due date; separate filings (FBAR, 3520) noted
- [ ] Open client items and assumptions clearly listed
