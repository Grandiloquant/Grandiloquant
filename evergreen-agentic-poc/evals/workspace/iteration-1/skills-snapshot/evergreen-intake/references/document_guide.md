# Document guide - what to pull from each form, and where the traps hide

Organised by form. For each: **extract** = fields to capture; **watch** = things the scanner (and a hurried preparer) miss.

## Wages
**W-2** - extract boxes 1-6, 7 (SS tips), 8 (allocated tips - NOT in box 1), 10 (dependent care), 12 (all codes), 13, 14, 15-20.
Watch: duplicate W-2s (reprints, phone photos); two employers for one person (excess SS); box 12 codes - D/E/G/S
pre-tax deferrals already out of box 1, AA/BB Roth already in box 1, W = HSA incl. employee pre-tax deferral, V = NSO income
(already in box 1 - fix the broker's basis), DD informational, C = GTL in box 1; box 14 overtime (total pay vs. premium),
RSU income, 2% S-corp shareholder health insurance, state/local boxes (which cities? what got withheld?), a W-2 belonging
to someone else.

## Interest, dividends, brokerage
**1099-INT** - boxes 1, 3 (US obligations - state-exempt), 4, 6, 8 (tax-exempt), 9, 10 (market discount), 11/12/13 (bond premium).
**1099-DIV** - 1a, 1b, 2a-2f, 3 (nondividend - not income), 4, 5 (199A), 7/8 (foreign tax/country), 9/10 (liquidation), 12, 13.
**1099-B / consolidated 1099** - read EVERY page: summary, then transaction detail (covered/noncovered, codes W/D/B, missing
basis), then supplemental pages (accrued interest paid on purchases, bond premium detail, foreign tax by country, % of income
from US obligations by state, margin interest, investment fees, WHFIT/UIT grantor-trust sections that sit outside the totals).
Watch: an original AND a corrected consolidated 1099; basis "not reported" or $0 for RSU/ESPP/NSO shares; wash sales the
broker can't see (spouse's account, IRA); crypto 1099-DA (2025 = proceeds only); IRA statements in the pile (not taxable).
**1099-K / 1099-NEC / 1099-MISC** - the same income can appear on both a 1099-NEC and a 1099-K; 1099-K gross ≠ net; 1099-K for
personal items sold at a loss; staking rewards on 1099-MISC that also appear in a crypto tax report.

## Retirement and benefits
**1099-R** - 1, 2a, 2b, 4, 5, 6 (NUA), 7 (codes: 1 early, 2 early-exception, 4 death, 7 normal, G direct rollover, H Roth rollover,
Y QCD reported, J/T Roth). Watch: corrected forms; QCDs (age 70½+); rollovers; Roth conversions (need Form 8606 + year-end
traditional IRA balances - check 5498s); QDRO payments; inherited IRAs.
**SSA-1099** - box 3, 4 (repaid), 5 (net), 6 (withholding), and the description area (Medicare Part B/D premiums).
**1099-G** - unemployment; state refund (taxable only if itemized AND it gave a benefit - needs prior-year Schedule A).
**1099-SA / 5498-SA / 5498** - HSA/IRA contributions and year-end values (pro-rata rule, excess contributions).
**1099-Q / 1098-T / 1098-E** - education; scholarships in 1098-T box 5 reduce qualified expenses.

## Property and debt
**1098** - interest, outstanding principal, origination date (pre-12/16/2017 = $1M limit), **property address** (a rental
address means Schedule E, not Schedule A), points, MIP, escrowed taxes. Lender statements for car loans (2025 Sch 1-A: VIN,
new/used, purchase date). **1099-S** - home sale. **1099-C** - cancelled debt (insolvency?). Closing statements, appraisals,
insurance claim letters (casualty - federally declared?).

## Pass-throughs and trusts
**K-1 (1065 / 1120-S / 1041)** - every box AND every statement/footnote: basis info, distributions (16D/19A), liabilities share
and its change, AMT items (17A), 199A statement (QBI, W-2 wages, UBIA, SSTB flag), state apportionment schedule, PTET credit,
property distributions, §704(c) notes, "final" box, suspended losses on termination, K-2/K-3 foreign items, draft vs final.

## Foreign
NR-4 (Canada), Indian bank interest certificates (often April-March fiscal years), foreign trust beneficiary statements,
1042-S (issued to someone the payer thinks is foreign), foreign account statements (max balance for FBAR/8938).

## Self-employment and rentals
P&Ls and general ledgers (accrual vs cash, personal items, meals, gifts, dues, equipment), mileage/tip logs (handwritten),
rental ledgers (repairs vs improvements, personal-use days, average stay length), Airbnb/Uber tax summaries.

## Correspondence
Emails, interview memos, phone notes: new dependents, marriages/separations, moves, missed estimates, documents promised,
elections made (83(b)), client decisions (SEP funding, MFJ vs MFS). Treat facts here as evidence - cite them.
